The Problem

Construction bookkeeping is drowning in manual data entry

Double entry, double errors

Every job cost gets typed into Procore, then retyped into QuickBooks. One wrong class code and the entire job cost report is off. Your team spends hours fixing entries that should never have been wrong.

Cost codes never match

Procore uses one numbering system. QuickBooks uses another. Your team maintains a translation spreadsheet that is always out of date. New cost codes fall through the cracks every month.

Overruns spotted too late

By the time someone compares budget to actual, the job is already over. The GC finds out at month-end, not when the problem starts. You look like the firm that missed it.

Step 1

Connect QuickBooks + Procore

OAuth login to both platforms. JobLedger reads your chart of accounts, cost codes, and active jobs. No IT team required, no API keys to manage.

Step 2

JobLedger suggests mappings based on your existing data. Review, adjust, and lock them in. From here on, every cost hits the right QuickBooks class automatically.

Timesheets, POs, and sub invoices flow from Procore to QuickBooks job reports. Budget variances surface instantly. Your team stops re-keying and starts advising.
